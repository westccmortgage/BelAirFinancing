What the request form collects

When you send the financing review form, we receive what you type into it: your name and email address, your phone number if you add one, and the optional details about the property and loan (area, estimated value, loan purpose, estimated loan amount, borrower profile, property use, preferred structure, and your message). The form is not a loan application, and you should not enter Social Security numbers, account numbers or similar sensitive identifiers in it.

The form also sends the web address of the page you used and the first page you visited on this site, and, if you arrived from an ad or a tracked link, the campaign details in that link (such as UTM values or a Google click ID). These are kept in your browser’s local storage so they can be attached to the form.

How we use it

We use this information to follow up about your request. That includes calls and email to the contact details you give us. We also use it to understand which campaigns lead to requests.

Google Ads tags

This site uses Google Ads tags. They measure visits and form submissions, may set cookies or similar identifiers, and may be used to show ads for our services on other websites. You can manage or opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
